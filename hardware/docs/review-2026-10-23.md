### Fix proposal for E12 (needs a layout change on both variants)

1. **Fast switchover (XREF).** CP2 gets a divider from `+5V_BUCK`: R27 30k / R28 10k gives 1.20–1.27 V, which is ≥ VREF 1.10 max and enables tFSW = 5 µs. Change R24 from 30k to 24k: PR1 is then ≥ 1.41 V at AUX 4.95 V, above CP2. Behaviour: AUX hands over below about 4.1–4.3 V. With KNX absent, CP2 = 0 and PR1 is above VREF, so IN1 (AUX) is selected. With AUX absent, IN2 (KNX) is selected. With this alone the test computes a 3.75 V minimum (pass).
2. **Bulk on the mux output.** C23 10 µF/0603 → 47–100 µF (1206 X5R, or polymer). This gives margin for the repeated handovers during TX bursts (TMR foldback/recovery chatter). The TMR 1 allows 1680 µF.
3. **Source status.** TPS2121 ST is open drain, high = IN1. Connect it to the spare D3/GPIO21 with a 10k pull-up to 3V3, instead of GND. Firmware can then pick `wifi_power_save`: continuous on AUX, modem sleep on KNX (E6). It can also tell a KNX outage from imminent core loss (E14).
4. **AUX-only with WiFi.** Option a: TMR 2411 (2 W, 400 mA, SIP-8 21.8 × 9.1 × 11.2 mm, 1000 µF max load); this needs new placement in both cases. Option b: limit the WiFi TX power in firmware while ST reports AUX without KNX, then measure. User decision.

New parts get new fixed references (R27, R28), so nothing renumbers. Freerouting is chaotic: expect re-placement around U10 (AGENTS.md).

### Follow-up (user decision, same day): 2 W converter on v3 instead of the switching change

in the free space of the front protrusion between the main board edge and the UI board.

- **Part:** U9 is now a TRACO TMR 2411. Ratings: 18–36 V in, 5 V, 400 mA, 1.6 kVDC functional, 1000 µF max load, Remote pin 3 left open (= on). Package SIP-8, 21.8 × 9.1 × 11.2 mm. Stock: Mouser 495-TMR-2411 (447 pcs, USD 15), Digi-Key 1951-2741-ND (1241 pcs, USD 17.24). The TMR 3-2411 (3 W, 600 mA) uses the same package and pins.

- **Physics:** 298 mA TX peak at 5 V against 400 mA. New test `test_aux_converter_carries_the_wifi_tx_peak` passes on v3; it is a strict xfail on v2, which keeps the TMR 1-2411.
- **Layout, v3 main:**
  - The board gets a tongue: outline x −10.3…0, y 18.3…59.7. It reaches enclosure z 52.2, which is 0.2 mm inside the protrusion walls and 0.5 mm behind the UI board's parts.
  - U9 stands on the tongue's upper half, pin 1 at the top, pin row at x −4.55. Its body (x −10.15…1.05) stays clear of the UI board's FFC exit above UI J1.

- **v3 tongue:** below U9 only a 1.4 mm strip remains (outline x −1.4…0, y 18.3…37.3), which frees the protrusion under U9 for the UI cable. With about 30 % of the capacitance left at 5 V (series curve estimate), the worst case (AUX lost at a 298 mA TX peak, 100 µs switchover) dips to 3.08 V, against 1.12 V with 10 µF. The 3.5 V target is still missed, so the test stays a strict xfail. At the continuous-RX load (115 mA) the dip is about 0.3 V.
- **v2 converter:** the TMR 2411 does not fit v2.
  - The 7.7 mm strip on the back is too narrow for its 9.1 mm body.
  - Under the interface board there is 11.0 mm, against an 11.7 mm maximum height.
  - The free-looking top area on the front sits over the REC3 (U3, back, y 38–70), so through-hole pins there would land on its body. It is also SELV-AUX/DALI copper, far from the KNX zone that the output must reach.

  U9 on v2 is therefore a TRACO **TRN 3-2411** (3 W, 5 V 600 mA, 18–36 V, 1.6 kVDC functional, current limit 180 % typ, 2200 µF max load). Its SIP is 11.9 × 7.7 × 11.0 mm with pins 1 −Vin, 2 +Vin, 3 +Vout, 5 −Vout. It stands in the old strip on the back (pin row y 33.6), with Fab/courtyard covering 4.0 mm on both sides of the pin row. Pins 2 (AUX) and 3 (KNX) are only 2.8 mm apart, so the AUX tracks leave upwards and the KNX tracks downwards. A 1.0 × 3.0 mm board slot between those pins lengthens the creepage path past 2 mm. Stock: Newark 47AC2160 (37), LCSC C5987491 (9), about USD 22, 24-week factory lead time. Check stock before ordering.
- **Tooling bug found (fixed):** the generated DRC rules tried to exempt an isolator's own pins with `A.Reference == 'Ux'`. KiCad evaluates creepage per net pair, so no item condition (`Reference`, `memberOfFootprint`) ever matches, and the exemption never worked. Previous boards never needed it. `layout.dru_rules` no longer pretends to exempt anything. `test_drc_creepage::test_slot_between_isolator_pins_meets_creepage` shows that a slot clears the violation and a rule cannot. `test_isolation.py` keeps its own same-isolator exemption.

## Physics answers (skill §4)

- **DALI current, internal supply:** JP1 fitted, limited by Q-pass/R 3R3 to 167–227 mA. **External supply:** limited by the supply (≤ 250 mA). The TX clamp at 275–350 mA never decides, and every TX path pulls below 4.5 V (`test_tx_low_level_at_250ma`). **Unpowered MCU:** the TX opto is off, so the bus is released. A sustained short is held by firmware only (E8/E14).
- **Opto edges:** TX +38 µs typical on active half-bits (E13). RX: bus falling edges are seen about 30 µs late and rising edges about 7.5 µs late, so received lows look about 22 µs shorter. That stays inside the 333–500 µs window for a 400–433 µs sender.
- **KNX:** VFILT ≥ 16.75 V at a 21 V bus and 40 mA. Buck on ≤ 15.7 V, off ≥ 10.1 V (tests). Modem-sleep WiFi down to a 16.3 V bus; continuous WiFi from 22.7 V. A TX burst on KNX-only power draws about 72 mA net from the 470 µF: 0.3 V droop per 2 ms. SAVEB warning, estimated with the firmware's 14 V trigger down to the buck's 10.1 V off point: about 50 ms at modem-sleep load and about 12 ms during TX. Bench-check the NVS flush time.
- **Inputs:** ADC ladder 2.2 / 1.65 / 1.32 V within the ESP32-C6 ADC (12 dB). SAVEB is low whenever KNX is off (E14). DALI_RX idles low with bus power and high without. I2C 4k7 in parallel with the module pull-ups is ≤ 1.4 mA. LED data at 3.3 V meets the AHCT VIH of 2.0 V.
- **Thermal (closed case):** REC3 about 0.4 W, TMR 1 ≤ 0.3 W, NCN5130 0.2 W, Q-pass 0.05 W idle (2–3 W only during active lows). In total about 1 W, which is moderate even in 1M.
