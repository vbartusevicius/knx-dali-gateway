## Power architecture: AUX-powered core (user decision, implemented on v3 rev D and v2 rev C)

The user made AUX the primary core supply, keeping the KNX fallback.

- **Circuit** (`hardware/circuits/blocks/core_power.py`):
  - The protected AUX rail (after the PTC, reverse diode and TVS) feeds a TRACO TMR 1-2411 (U9): 18-36 V in, 5 V ±1 % at 200 mA, 1.5 kVDC functional isolation between SELV-AUX and SELV-KNX.
  - A TI TPS2121 priority mux (U10) selects it as IN1 while its PR1 divider (30k/10k) is above the 1.10 V reference, i.e. AUX above 4.4 V. It falls back to the KNX buck (IN2, still 4.94 V) within microseconds.
  - Both inputs are reverse blocked, so the two 5 V sources never feed each other, whatever their tolerances.
  - Plain LM66100 ORing was rejected: TI's make-before-break ORing needs at least 250 mV between the sources, and the SCT2400 feedback tolerance (0.79-0.83 V) cannot guarantee that.
- **Why not AUX-only:** confirmed requirement. The gateway must still run and be ETS-programmable from red/black alone, e.g. with an external DALI PSU and no AUX. The NCN5130 cannot run from AUX.
- **Why isolated:** KNX TP must stay floating. A direct AUX- to KNX- tie would bypass the supply's bus choke (MDT STC unchoked U2) or tie the bus to an arbitrary 24 V supply.
- **Budget:**
  - The core draws about 115 mA at 5 V with continuous WiFi RX, within the converter's 200 mA (`test_aux_converter_carries_the_sustained_core_load`).
  - TX peaks above 200 mA drag the AUX rail below the switchover threshold, and the mux hands those peaks to the KNX path (470 µF store).
  - KNX-only: modem-sleep WiFi works down to a 16.3 V bus; continuous WiFi needs at least 22.7 V.
- **v3 placement:**
  - U9 stands in the rear-bottom corner beside the AUX input. Its pins 1/2 sit in the AUX zone and pins 4/6 in a new SELV-KNX island, with 2 mm keepouts.
  - F1, D3 and D4 moved. The XIAO moved 0.6 mm up.
  - The base's right wall has a 0.4 mm recess over U9's top (11.0 ±0.5 mm), generated from the part data (`front_part_reliefs`).
  - The protrusion tab the user suggested was not needed.
- **v2 placement (rev C):**
  - U9 stands on the back of v2 main, in the 7.7 mm strip between the interface socket tails (J6, y 28.4) and the REC3 body (y 38.0). Its 11.0 mm height fits the space under the board.
  - Pins 1/2 sit in a small SELV-AUX island joined to the AUX zone above; pins 4/6 sit in SELV-KNX, with 2 mm keepouts.
  - The supply-off optocoupler U4 moved to x 18.4 (with its slot), between U9 pin 6 and C4's leads. C4 moved 1 mm to make room.
  - The TPS2121 mux and its passives sit on the front, next to J4.
  - The interface board did not have to move.

## Verification items without a fix (bench)

- v3 FFC: Type B (opposite-side contacts) and pin 1 to pin 1 depend on the fold. Make a paper mock-up before plugging in (rework-rev-c.md step 5).
- KF142R release buttons: their orientation inside the closed case is not verified. Insert wires with ferrules; releasing one may need the case open.
- XIAO pre-soldered header pins (about 6 mm) in 5.0 mm low-profile sockets: check that they seat fully. The 11.0 mm stack is modelled on that assumption.
- PST 1,0 strip plastic height versus the v3 inner wall at x = -1.3 (0.1 mm gap modelled from a 2.8 mm "length" figure).
- DALI timing with the oscilloscope: TX half-bits 400–433 µs and RX skew. Firmware has no edge compensation yet.
- KNX power at the real bus voltage with WiFi active (E6). KNX reception on v3 rev C (L1).
