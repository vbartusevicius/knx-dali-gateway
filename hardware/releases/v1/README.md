# KNX-DALI gateway hardware v1 (POC, 3 DIN)

Generated by `hardware/scripts/release.py v1` from `hardware/layout/poc/`. The
script only writes a board when KiCad DRC reports no errors and no unrouted
connection, every part has an LCSC number or a hand-fit part number, and every
JLC-assembled part passes `hardware/scripts/jlc_parts.py`: its manufacturer
part, value and voltage rating match the circuit, there is stock for 5 boards,
and JLCPCB's own (EasyEDA) footprint for that LCSC number lands pin for pin on
our pads. The placement file's rotations and centres come from that fit.
CI (`hardware/tests/test_release_current.py`) fails as soon as the boards no
longer match this release.

## Ordering at JLCPCB

1. Upload `<board>_gerbers.zip`. Main: 4 layers, 1.6 mm, standard JLC04161H-7628
   stack-up, 0.2 mm minimum hole (the NCN5130 thermal vias). UI: 2 layers, 1.6 mm.
2. Assembly: `<board>_bom_jlc.csv` and `<board>_cpl_jlc.csv`. The through-hole
   parts in the BOM (C4, J2, J3, JP1) can go to JLC's THT service or be
   hand-soldered.
3. `<board>_assembly_check.csv` lists every JLC part with JLCPCB's footprint,
   KiCad's and JLCPCB's rotation and the stock. Still glance at JLC's
   placement preview for the parts on the bottom side (main: C3, C4, C16, F1,
   L1, Q1, Q2, R1, R2, R10, R11, U4, U5; UI: J1): their rotation follows
   JLCPCB's documented bottom convention (footprint mirrored about its own x
   axis), which is the one rule the check cannot read from JLCPCB's data.
   Pin 1 / polarity marks to look for: Q1-Q6 and U1/U2/U6 pin 1, the D1-D9
   cathode bands, the D5 bridge's + corner, C4's minus stripe.

## Hand-fitted parts (`<board>_hand_fit.csv`)

- Main J1: Phoenix 1574200 KNX pin strip (Mouser 651-1574200).
- Main U3: RECOM REM3-2415S/A isolated converter (DIP-24, A pinning).
- Main U7: Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 on two 1x7 female headers, 8.5 mm (LCSC C54876729).
- Main JP1: two 2.54 mm jumper caps (LCSC C100114).
- UI J2: 0.96" SSD1306 I2C OLED module with pin order GND, VCC, SCL, SDA.
- UI to main: 10-pin 1.0 mm JST-SH cable, pin 1 to pin 1.

## DALI supply jumpers (JP1)

Both caps fitted: the gateway powers the DALI line from the AUX input
(about 14.5 V, current-limited to about 180 mA). Both removed: the line needs
its own DALI power supply; the gateway then only talks on it.

## First power-up

1. No AUX, JP1 caps removed, no USB. Connect the KNX bus only.
2. Measure VFILT (about 20-28 V), +3V3_TRX (3.3 V) and +5V (about 4.9 V).
3. Fit the XIAO and check it boots over USB first, then from KNX power.
4. Connect 24-30 V to AUX, fit both JP1 caps, measure the DALI line (about 14.5 V idle).

Do not connect USB and KNX at the same time unless the laptop is isolated
(battery or USB isolator).

## Verified for this release

- KiCad DRC with the project rules: 0 errors, 0 unrouted, both boards.
- Isolation measured on the copper of all layers: DALI-KNX 6.60 mm,
  DALI-AUX 6.82 mm, KNX-AUX 7.00 mm (required 6 / 6 / 2 mm).
- Every symbol pin maps to a footprint pad; every polarised part's pin order
  was checked against its datasheet (SCT2400, BC847/857, MJD45H11, IRLML0060,
  TLP383, LM66100, REM3, NCN5130, SMAJ TVS, LEDs).
- Every JLC part checked against JLCPCB's part record and EasyEDA footprint
  (manufacturer part, value, voltage rating, stock, pin-for-pin landing);
  D5 (DF06S: datasheet pins 1 +, 2 ~, 3 -, 4 ~) and SW1/SW2 (PTS645: 1-2 and
  3-4 are the contacts) use the cited pad maps in
  `hardware/product/jlc_pad_maps.yaml`. 19 parts need a JLCPCB rotation other
  than KiCad's; the placement file carries the fitted values.
- Enclosure (two printed parts, `enclosure/`), probed as assembled: no case
  material inside any part volume or either board, every opening clear,
  every support touching its board, rail channel per EN 60715, at least 2 mm
  of air between every part and the case (connectors that reach their
  openings excepted).

## Assembly

Off-board parts are in `assembly_parts.csv` (UI cable, XIAO sockets, jumper
caps, two case screws, printed parts).

1. UI board: solder the OLED module onto J2 on its 2.5 mm header spacer and
   trim the pins flush with the module board (the module sits in a pocket
   behind the front wall).
2. Main board: fit the XIAO sockets and the XIAO, both JP1 caps if the gateway
   should power the DALI line.
3. Drop the main board into the base onto its five legs.
4. Plug the 10-pin cable into main J4 and UI J1 (1:1, either way round). Both
   connectors are top-entry, so the cable runs straight between the boards.
5. Snap the UI board into the cover from behind, front first: its edges push
   the four sprung hooks in the side walls outwards, and they spring back
   behind the board once it rests against the four standoffs. To take it out,
   press the hooks outwards.
6. Close the cover over the base (its pegs hold the main board down) and fit
   the two 2.5 x 10 thread-forming screws from the top and bottom faces, the
   sides the wires come from, into the cover's screw tabs. Opening: the same
   two screws.

## DIN rail

The back is 5 mm deep with a channel for the TS35 rail. Hook the fixed top
hook over the rail's upper lip and press the bottom on: the latch lip's
lead-in pushes the sprung latch bar down until it snaps under the lower lip.
To remove, put a flat screwdriver into the hole in the latch tab that sticks
out below the case (straight in from the front), pull it down and tilt the
device off the rail. Two pairs of thin beams on either side of the bar are
its spring (`enclosure/renders/latch.png`).

## Buttons and LEDs

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| PRG/RST tap | Reboot (ESP32 and KNX stack restart, configuration kept) |
| PRG/RST held 5 s | KNX programming mode on/off; the KNX LED turns red |
| FUNC tap | Next display page; wakes the display |
| FUNC held 5 s | WiFi setup |
| Both held 10 s | Factory reset; all three LEDs blink red while held |

The three RGB LEDs never switch off. STATUS: green healthy (short blue blink
every 5 s when WiFi is connected), amber degraded, red fault, blue blinking in
WiFi setup. KNX: dim green idle, bright green on traffic, red in programming
mode, red blinking without bus. DALI: dim green idle, green on our frames,
cyan on other masters, amber gear fault, red no bus power, red blinking bus
short, blue during commissioning. Only the display times out.

## Connectors

The connectors are flush with the faces the wires come from: the DALI and
AUX terminals' wire-entry faces sit in openings in the top face (0.2 mm
short of it, because their pads need 0.3 mm to the board edge), and the KNX
plug's underside is flush with the bottom face, in a recess open to the front
and the bottom. The terminal screws are reached through holes in the top
shoulder; JP1 stays under the cover (`enclosure/renders/iso.png`,
`knx_recess.png`).

## Assumptions to check before printing the case

- OLED module geometry (board 27.3 x 27.8 mm, glass 26.7 x 19.3 mm, stack 5.5 mm
  above the UI board): taken from typical modules, no manufacturer drawing.
  Measure the module you buy; the values are in
  `hardware/circuits/variants/poc/layout_ui.yaml` (`oled_module`) and
  `mechanical/scripts/ui_stackup.py`.
- DALI/AUX terminals: rotated so their wire openings face the top wall
  (MaiXu MX126 drawing). Confirm on the first board before printing.
- The UI snap hooks (0.5 mm overlap, 6 mm wide tongues), the cover lip, the screw pilot (2.2 mm) and the rail latch
  (0.8 mm flexure beams, 2 mm travel, 1.5 mm lip) are first-print geometry;
  expect to adjust them after printing in your material.
- The status LEDs shine through a 0.6 mm skin of the front wall, each inside
  a baffle; print the cover in a light, translucent colour (natural PETG works)
  or the LEDs will not show.
