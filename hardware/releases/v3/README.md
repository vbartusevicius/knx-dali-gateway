# KNX-DALI gateway hardware v3 (1 DIN, four boards)

Generated by `HARDWARE_VARIANT=v3 python3 hardware/scripts/release.py v3` from
`hardware/layout/v3/`, with the same gates as v1/v2: KiCad DRC 0 errors and 0
unrouted, every part orderable or hand-fit, every JLC part checked against
JLCPCB's part record, live stock and its EasyEDA footprint (pin for pin; the
placement file carries the fitted rotations and centres). CI
(`HARDWARE_VARIANT=v3`, `test_release_current.py`) fails as soon as any board
no longer matches this release.

All four board-to-board links use the same 8-pin 0.5 mm dual-contact FFC
connector (Kinghelm `KH-FPC0.5-H1.2SMT-8P-QCHF`, LCSC `C48532769`) and 50 mm
flat cables (`C6936295`). The two DALI FFCs stay inside their own domains
(J2 carries only SELV logic, J3 carries only the isolated DALI supply).

## Ordering at JLCPCB

1. Upload `<board>_gerbers.zip`:
   - `main`, `knx`, `dali`: 4 layers, **1.2 mm**
   - `ui`: 4 layers, **1.6 mm**
2. PCB Assembly: `<board>_bom_jlc.csv` + `<board>_cpl_jlc.csv` (one-sided on
   `main`, both sides on `knx`, `dali` and `ui`).
3. `<board>_assembly_check.csv` lists JLCPCB footprint, rotation and stock for
   every part. Confirm the bottom-side parts and rotated parts in JLCPCB's
   placement preview (`knx` bottom: C4, C7, C10, R1-R3; `dali` bottom: D1, D2,
   D5, J3, Q3, Q4, R6, R7; `ui` bottom: C1-C6, J1, R1-R4, U1).

## Hand-fitted parts (`<board>_hand_fit.csv`)

- `main` U2: RECOM **REM3-2415S/A/CTRL** (DIP-24, A pinning with pin 1 CTRL;
  no minimum load required).
- `main` U5: Seeed **XIAO ESP32C6 Pre-Soldered (SKU 102010636)** in two 1x7
  5.0 mm female headers (`assembly_parts.csv`), USB-C facing the front tip.
- `dali` J1: **WAGO 2601-1102** 2-pole 3.5 mm lever push-in terminal (horizontal
  wire entry from the top face, levers operated from the top shoulder).
- `main` J1, J2: two **Phoenix Contact 1574200** (PST 1,0/2-5,75) pin strips
  on the bottom shoulder of `main` (pins pointing +x to the right), mated from the right side by the red/black KNX plug (`1574300`,
  front) and the yellow/grey 20-32 V AUX plug (`1574299`, rear).
- `ui` J2: Winstar **WEA012832FWPP3N00000** (WEA012832F, 0.91" 128x32 SSD1306
  I2C, mounted vertically; pin order GND, VIN, SCL, SDA, datasheet p.3).

Off-board parts are in `assembly_parts.csv` (four 50 mm 8-pin 0.5 mm FFC
cables, XIAO 5.0 mm sockets, red/black KNX plug, yellow/grey AUX plug, two JP1
jumper caps, two 2.5 x 10 thread-forming screws, the two printed case parts).
