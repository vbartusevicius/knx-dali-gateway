# KNX-DALI gateway hardware v2 (2 DIN, three boards)

Generated by `HARDWARE_VARIANT=v2 python3 hardware/scripts/release.py` from
`hardware/layout/v2/`, with the same gates as v1: KiCad DRC 0 errors and 0
unrouted, every part orderable or hand-fit, every JLC part checked against
JLCPCB's part record, live stock and its EasyEDA footprint (pin for pin; the
placement file carries the fitted rotations and centres). CI
(`HARDWARE_VARIANT=v2`, `test_release_current.py`) fails as soon as the boards
no longer match this release.

| Board | Size | Layers | Position in the case | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main | 31.5 x 76.5 mm | 4 | flat in the base: KNX, 5 V, isolated DALI supply, all terminals | `main/` |
| Interface | 31.5 x 27.5 mm | 4 | plugged onto main (11 mm above it): isolated DALI TX/RX | `interface/` |
| UI | 31.5 x 40.5 mm | 4 | snapped into the cover: XIAO ESP32-C6, OLED, 2 buttons, 3 RGB LEDs | `ui/` |

The interface board has no case retention of its own: its two male headers
(J1 logic, J2 DALI, on its back) plug into main J6/J5 (8.5 mm sockets +
2.5 mm header plastic = 11.0 mm). DALI TX/RX reach it from the XIAO through
the main-to-UI cable (pins 7/8) and main J6. The only cable is that 10-pin
JST-SH jumper.

## Ordering at JLCPCB

1. Upload `<board>_gerbers.zip`: 4 layers, 1.6 mm, all three boards
   (standard JLC04161H-7628 stack-up; main has 0.2 mm thermal vias).
2. PCB Assembly, both sides: `<board>_bom_jlc.csv` + `<board>_cpl_jlc.csv`.
   Through-hole parts in the BOMs (main J5/J6 sockets and JP1, interface J1/J2
   headers on its back, terminals J2/J3) go to JLC's THT service.
3. `<board>_assembly_check.csv` lists JLCPCB footprint, rotation and stock for
   every part. Glance at the placement preview for the bottom-side parts
   (main: C3, L1, R1-R3; interface: C1, D1, D2, J1, J2, R4, R5, R7; UI: C5,
   C7, J1); their rotation follows JLCPCB's documented bottom convention.
   Pin 1 / polarity: optocouplers, transistors, the bridge's + corner, SK6805
   corner marks, diode bands.

## Front legend (optional print)

Three flat faces carry the legend: bottom shoulder (KNX + -), front (PRG/RST,
FUNC, LED names and rings) and top shoulder (- + "PSU 20-32V" and DA DA "BUS", enclosed by one bracket
with DALI in its bar: this input feeds only the internal DALI bus supply,
not the gateway). 20-32 V keeps margin to the REM3 start-up (18 V max plus
the SS16 reverse-polarity drop) and to the 33 V PTC/TVS limit; a 24 V
supply or the MDT STC-1280.01 30 V output are both inside.

- `enclosure/front_legend.pdf` (also `.svg`): the front view at 1:1, black =
  print, cyan = face edges and openings for positioning only. For UV/pad
  printing on an FDM cover (custom quote) or an adhesive polyester label cut
  along the cyan lines.
- `enclosure/cover_embossed.stl (raised embossed text) or cover_color.3mf` or `enclosure/cover_color_obj.zip` (OBJ + MTL):
  the cover for full-colour resin printing (JLC3DP "Full-Color Resin", WJP).
  Upload either one instead of `cover.stl` and select full-colour printing.
  The legend is a separate black body 0.3 mm deep, flush with the faces; the
  cover is light grey. WJP resin is rigid and JLC3DP recommends walls >= 1 mm
  (snap features >= 2 mm): the cover's 0.6 mm LED skins, 0.8 mm baffles and the
  sprung snap tongues are below that, and opaque white resin passes less LED
  light than translucent PETG. Ask for their review, or print the cover in
  PETG and use the PDF instead.

## Hand-fitted parts (`<board>_hand_fit.csv`)

- Main J1: Phoenix 1574200 KNX pin strip.
- Main U3: RECOM REM3-2415S/A/CTRL (DIP-24, A pinning with pin 1 CTRL; no minimum load required).
- Main C4: 470 uF 35 V, D10 x 16 (SamYoung KMG35V470M10*16 LO, LCSC C165500), **lying down
  on the back**: bend both leads 90 deg 3 mm from the body, insert from the
  back so the body axis is 7 mm below the board (2 mm air gap) and runs
  towards the KNX end, solder on the front, trim. It rests in two cradles in
  the base; the leads do not carry it. Minus stripe to pad 2 (round pad).
- UI U2: Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 **Pre-Soldered (SKU 102010636)**, detachable in
  two 1x7 8.5 mm female headers soldered to the back of the UI board (the bare
  113991254 ships without pin headers); module 11 mm above the board. Silkscreen marks
  USB, D0 and 5V. For USB flashing unsnap the UI board (it stays on its cable)
  or pull the XIAO out; with the board in the cover the protrusion wall
  blocks a straight USB-C plug.
- UI J2: Winstar **WEA012864DWPP3N00003** (WEA012864D-03, 0.96" 128x64
  SSD1306 I2C, white; `...DLPP...` yellow, `...DBPP...` sky blue), pin order
  VCC, GND, SCL, SDA. Do not use generic modules: their size, glass position
  and pin order differ. Drawing: WEA012864D-03 datasheet p.3.

Off-board parts are in `assembly_parts.csv` (10-pin JST-SH 1:1 cable, KNX bus
plug Phoenix PTS 0,5/2-PH-5,75 BKRD, XIAO sockets, two JP1 jumper caps, two
2.5 x 10 thread-forming screws, the two printed parts).

## Assembly

1. Main board: C4 lying down as above, both JP1 caps if the gateway should
   power the DALI line. Plug the interface board onto J5/J6 (it only fits one
   way: J1 at the KNX end).
2. Drop the main board into the base: legs underneath, C4 in its cradles.
3. UI board: fit the XIAO sockets and the XIAO.
4. OLED: lay the module face down into the cover's pocket (glass through the
   window). Push the UI board over its four pins and snap it in behind the
   four hooks. Then solder the four OLED pins from the back of the UI board.
   The pocket, not the header, sets the glass flush with the front.
5. Plug the 10-pin cable into main J4 and UI J1.
6. Close the cover (its pegs hold the main board down) and fit the two screws
   from the top and bottom faces.

## DALI supply jumpers (JP1)

Both caps fitted: the gateway powers the DALI line from the AUX input. Both
removed: the line needs its own DALI supply. JP1 is reached with the cover off,
beside the interface board.

## Buttons and LEDs

Same as v1 (PRG/RST tap reboot, hold 5 s programming mode; FUNC tap next page,
hold 5 s WiFi setup; both 10 s factory reset; LEDs never switch off). The XIAO
pin map is unchanged, so the v1 firmware applies.

## Verified for this release

- KiCad DRC with the project rules: 0 errors, 0 unrouted, all three boards.
- Isolation measured on the copper of all layers: main DALI-KNX 6.30 mm,
  DALI-AUX 6.12 mm, KNX-AUX 25.3 mm; interface DALI-KNX 6.76 mm (required 6 /
  6 / 2 mm). Isolation slots under both interface optocouplers and across the
  main board's KNX/DALI boundary.
- Board-to-board: header and socket pins coincide pin for pin (in enclosure
  coordinates) and carry the same nets; the cable carries the same net on
  pin n at both ends, as listed in the spec.
- Every part on all three boards, hand-fitted ones included, checked against
  a supplier or manufacturer reference: JLCPCB's EasyEDA footprint must land
  pin for pin on ours and our symbol's pin names must match JLCPCB's symbol
  (XIAO via Seeed's JLCPCB entry C9900124963, C4 via C165500); the REM3
  converter, Phoenix KNX pin strip and Winstar OLED are checked against the
  pinouts cited from their datasheets (`hardware/product/pinouts.yaml`).
  JLC-assembled parts also for manufacturer part, value, rating and live stock.
- XIAO modelled 11 mm above the board (socket + header plastic) in every fit
  check.
- Front legend labels lie on their faces, clear of every opening, with the
  polarity marks over the right pins (`mechanical/tests/test_front_legend.py`).
- Enclosure probed as assembled with all three boards: no case material in
  any part or board, no part of one board inside another board or its parts,
  the cable path crosses no third board, the OLED fits its pocket with the
  header gap (3.13 mm) above the parts under it, C4 sits in its cradles,
  every support touches its board.
- STEP models of all three boards export with every 3D model resolved and
  aligned with its courtyard (including C4 lying down).
